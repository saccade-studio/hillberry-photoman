How PhotoMan keeps your photos intact

Quality & color

  • Full resolution. Photos are never resized. Only the crop you choose is removed.
  • Maximum JPEG quality with full color detail (4:4:4). PNG export is fully lossless.
  • Same color space. The photo's embedded profile (Adobe RGB, Display P3, sRGB…) is read without conversion and written back unchanged. The sRGB watermark is converted into that space so its colors stay exact.
  • Camera info and credits are kept. Rotation and size fields are corrected, and embedded previews of the uncropped, un-watermarked original are removed.

Scott Print Sizes

  • 8 × 10 in and 13 × 19 in are the sizes that can be printed on site. The Crop panel shows the print resolution for the area you've chosen.
